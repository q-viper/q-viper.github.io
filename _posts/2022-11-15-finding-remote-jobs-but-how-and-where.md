Finding remote jobs can be challenging: there are many opportunities, but finding the right fit for your skills and profile takes effort. This post lists popular platforms, practical tips, and suggestions based on my personal experience.

## Why remote jobs?

Remote work became mainstream after COVID-19, and it offers several advantages:

* Reduced commute time — save hours previously spent getting to and from the office.
* Flexibility — choose where and when you work if the role allows it.
* Better work-life fit — work from locations that minimize distractions and suit your routine.

## Prepare before applying

A strong portfolio and a clear profile are essential for remote work. Showcase relevant projects, list technologies, and include concise case studies or links to code. If you need help getting started with monetizing programming skills, see: [Making money with programming](https://wp.me/pdCti4-ln).

## Top platforms to find remote jobs

Note: the list below reflects my personal experience and may not match everyone’s results.

### 1. Upwork

I started on Upwork in 2021. At first I had little success because my profile and cover letters were weak. After improving them I started getting jobs and earned nearly $2,000 in one month. Upwork’s flexible payment methods and low fees are appealing.

![Upwork Logo]({{site.url}}/assets/freelancing/where_to/upwork.png)

Image from Andrew Debell on [Pros and Cons of Upwork](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/pros-cons-using-upwork-freelancer-andrew-debell/).

Tips for Upwork
* Create a detailed profile with a professional photo, clear summary, and portfolio links.
* Start with smaller jobs and competitive rates to build reviews.
* Request testimonials from satisfied clients.
* Monitor your job success score and strive to meet deadlines.
* Read community forums like r/upwork for practical advice.

### 2. Fiverr

Fiverr is great for selling defined "gigs" — packages of services you can repeat. It works well for designers, writers, video editors, and other creators.

![Fiverr Logo]({{site.url}}/assets/freelancing/where_to/fiverr.png)

Image from the digest.myhq.in on [How to make money on Fiverr?](https://digest.myhq.in/how-make-money-on-fiverr/)

Tips for Fiverr
* Define a clear niche and create gigs with strong keywords.
* Use high-quality images and a concise, benefit-oriented description.

### 3. Freelancer.com

Freelancer.com has many listings but also scams. Once you build credibility, it can be a good source of consistent work.

![Freelancer Logo]({{site.url}}/assets/freelancing/where_to/freelancer.png)

Image from Websiteplanet.com on [Review: Working as a Freelancer on Freelancer.com](https://www.websiteplanet.com/blog/review-working-freelancer-freelancer-com/)

Tips for Freelancer.com
* Be cautious of low-paying or suspicious jobs.
* Maintain an up-to-date portfolio and relevant certifications.

### 4. Toptal

Toptal focuses on high-quality talent and usually targets experienced professionals. The screening process is rigorous and typically requires strong technical skills.

![Toptal Logo]({{site.url}}/assets/freelancing/where_to/toptal.png)

Image from volxompvtltd.com on [The Complete Guide to Toptal Screening Interview](https://volxompvtltd.com/toptal-initial-screening-interview-guide/)

Tips for Toptal
* Prepare for technical interviews and algorithmic challenges (e.g., LeetCode).
* Toptal typically expects at least two years of industry experience.

### 5. Turing

Turing is similar to Toptal: you must pass technical screenings to access high-quality remote roles. They also post many openings on LinkedIn.

![Turing Logo]({{site.url}}/assets/freelancing/where_to/turing.png)

Tips for Turing
* Brush up on data structures, algorithms, and your tech stack.
* Be patient; the screening process can take time.

### 6. Wellfound (formerly AngelList)

Wellfound lists startup jobs and is useful for developers who want to join growing teams. Complete your profile and tailor applications to each company.

![WellFound Logo]({{site.url}}/assets/freelancing/where_to/wellfound.png)

Image from AngelList.com

Tips for Wellfound
* Fill out your profile completely and include links to projects or code samples.
* Write a concise cover note highlighting how you can help the startup.

### 7. Remotive

Remotive aggregates remote roles with good filters for role type, location, and salary.

![Remotive Logo]({{site.url}}/assets/freelancing/where_to/remotive.png)

Image from [remotive.com](https://remotive.com/)

Tips for Remotive
* Use filters and set up alerts for relevant positions.
* Tailor applications and provide a clear CV.

### 8. Working Nomads

Working Nomads is a job board for digital nomads and remote professionals. It aggregates listings from various companies.

![Working Nomad Logo]({{site.url}}/assets/freelancing/where_to/working_nomad.png)

Image from [workingnomad.com](https://www.workingnomads.com/)

### 9. We Work Remotely

We Work Remotely categorizes jobs by role and tech stack, making it easy to find openings in your area of expertise.

![We Work Remotely Logo]({{site.url}}/assets/freelancing/where_to/we_work_remotely.png)

Image from [weworkremotely.com](https://weworkremotely.com/)

### 10. FlexJobs

FlexJobs curates remote and flexible job listings; it is a paid service but is known for higher-quality, vetted postings.

![Flexjobs Logo]({{site.url}}/assets/freelancing/where_to/flexjobs.png)

Image from [Flexjobs.com](https://www.flexjobs.com/)

Honorable mentions
* Himalayas — job listings organized by company. ([himalayas.app](https://himalayas.app))
* Outsourcely — good for web development, copywriting, support, and UI/UX work. ([outsourcely.com](https://www.outsourcely.com/))
* Remote OK — aggregated remote listings. ([remoteok.com](https://remoteok.com/))
* Remote.co — curated remote jobs and resources. ([remote.co](https://remote.co/))

## Final tips for remote job hunting

* Tailor each application — one-size-fits-all cover letters rarely win.
* Keep your portfolio concise and evidence-based (links, case studies, screenshots).
* Start small to build reviews on freelance platforms.
* Network on LinkedIn and industry communities.
* Be persistent — finding the right remote role takes time.
